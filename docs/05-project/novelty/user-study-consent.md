# User-study consent template

Adapt this text to your organisation and jurisdiction. It is not legal advice.

## Study title

Judging live decision recommendations

## Purpose

We are evaluating whether a decision-support recommendation is timely, trustworthy and useful to
people who do the work.

## What you will do

- read 20 short scenarios;
- see two recommendations for each scenario;
- choose an action and rate each recommendation on timeliness, trust and workload;
- spend about 20–30 minutes.

## Data we collect

- your choices and ratings;
- an anonymous participant id;
- no name, email, phone number or free-text personal data.

## Risks

The scenarios are fictional and do not involve real customers or real incidents. The main risk is
fatigue from reading 40 short recommendations.

## Benefits

You help us improve how decision support is evaluated. There is no direct payment unless stated
separately.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time and skip any question. You can withdraw your
data before the analysis is frozen.

## Confidentiality

Results are reported in aggregate. An anonymous participant id links the two recommendations for the
same scenario; it is not linked to your identity.
