# User-study recruitment template

Adapt the wording to your organisation.

## Short message

Subject: 20 minutes to judge decision recommendations

Hi,

I am evaluating a decision-support layer for live operational decisions. I need 20–30 people for a
20–30 minute study. You will read 20 short scenarios, see two recommendations for each, choose what
you would do, and rate the recommendation.

No personal data is collected beyond anonymous choices and ratings. The scenarios are fictional.
Participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.

Would you have 20–30 minutes this week?

Thanks,
[Name]

## What to send before the session

- the consent form;
- the time and link or room;
- a note that the study takes 20–30 minutes;
- the fact that the scenarios are fictional.

## After the session

- do not collect names in the results file;
- store only the anonymous participant id;
- keep the consent records separately from the results;
- delete raw notes after the analysis is frozen.
